    Before you begin

    Granite Estate is an informational document-preparation tool. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and using it does not create an attorney–client relationship.

    It compares your draft documents against New Hampshire statutory reference points (NH RSA) and prepares an organized brief for a New Hampshire-licensed attorney, who should review and supervise the execution of any estate planning documents.

    This tool is designed for simple NH estates only. It will stop and refer you to an attorney if your situation appears outside that scope.
